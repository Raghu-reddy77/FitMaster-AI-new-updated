Biometric Parameters

📐 Mifflin-St Jeor Equation (BMR)
BMR = 10*Weight(kg) + 6.25*Height(cm) - 5*Age(y) + s

Clinically recognized as the most accurate non-calorimetric formula for estimating resting metabolic energy expenditure in healthy adults.

⚡ Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE)
TDEE = BMR × Activity Factor

Accounts for Non-Exercise Activity Thermogenesis (NEAT), exercise thermogenesis, and the Thermic Effect of Food (TEF).
